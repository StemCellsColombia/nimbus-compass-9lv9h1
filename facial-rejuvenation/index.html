What happens in the skin

It starts underneath.

Skin ages in its deeper layer, below what you see. That is where these cells are placed, and where they aim to work.

The skin’s builder cells

Your skin has cells that build its scaffolding, and they slow down with age. Mesenchymal cells release signals that may help them work more like they used to.

Collagen & elastin

Collagen gives skin its structure and elastin its snap-back. By supporting those builder cells, the cells aim to support both — which is why results build over weeks, not overnight.

The tiny blood vessels

Tiny vessels deep in the skin feed it. Cell signals may support that network, so the tissue receives more of what it needs to renew itself.

Your own PRP

The doctor draws a little of your blood and concentrates its platelets, rich in growth factors. It goes in together with the cells, and because it is yours, it is part of you.

Quick answers

Before you book.

How many days do I need in Medellín?

One day of treatment, with one night at the hotel in the same building as the treatment center. That is true for all three options.

What is the application like?

It is done by mesotherapy: many small injections in the face, similar to a botox session, with local anesthesia.

When will I see results?

Results are expected to appear from about the first month, and they build over the weeks that follow. Many patients add optional PRP once a month for three months to support them, quoted separately. Every skin responds at its own pace.

Will it remove my wrinkles?

The cells aim to support the quality of the skin: its collagen, its elastin, its elasticity. If expression lines are your main goal, tell the aesthetic doctor in your consultation, so your plan is designed around it.

What is facial rejuvenation designed for?

It is designed specifically for anti-aging: skin quality, firmness and a rested glow.

Where do I rest after the treatment?

In your room at the 4-star hotel, which is in the same building as the treatment center. Your coordinator and your doctor stay in touch with you, and your written instructions come with you when you fly home.

What is included in the price?

The cells and your PRP, the facial cleanse and vitamin C IV, two online consultations with the aesthetic doctor, a 4-star hotel for one night, airport pickup and all transfers, and a bilingual patient care coordinator. Flights and meals are not included.

Can I combine it with other treatments?

Yes. There is a combined face and hair option, and many patients add a joint or spine application to the same trip. The coordinator organizes the days so nothing collides.

What can you expect?

Every body responds at its own pace. What you can expect is the quality of the cells, the specialist who matches your case, a price with nothing hidden inside it, and a medical team with you the whole way.

Is this approved by the FDA?

Stem cell therapy is not approved by the FDA, but in Colombia it is legal, and that is where your treatment takes place, performed by specialist physicians.
