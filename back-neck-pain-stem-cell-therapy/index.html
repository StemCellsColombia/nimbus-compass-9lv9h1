Spine FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Can stem cells help me avoid spinal fusion surgery?

For many patients with herniated or bulging discs, sciatica or degenerative disc disease, stem cell therapy is a way to treat the spine before an irreversible fusion. A specialist reviews your imaging first, so your plan is designed for your spine.

Can stem cells really reach the disc?

Yes. With fluoroscopic guidance, the specialists apply intradiscally, epidurally and into the facet joints — reaching the exact structures causing your pain.

How many days do I need in Medellín?

The treatment itself is a single day. It's designed to feel like a focused business trip: airport pickup, hotel and treatment center in the same building, and a clear itinerary. The doctors recommend staying at least 2 days after your application to rest, and you can add extra nights to enjoy the city.

How much does spine stem cell therapy cost?

All-inclusive spine treatment in Medellín starts from $5,100 USD, including the cells, the fluoroscopy-guided application in the operating room, hotel, transfers and follow-up. Comparable treatments in the US typically run $20,000+.

Does it hurt?

The application is done under sedation, so you stay comfortable. Afterward, you rest at the hotel, one elevator away, with your coordinator checking on you and clear post-care instructions in hand.

When will I see results?

Improvement builds gradually, typically between 4 and 6 months after your treatment, and some patients keep improving at 8 months. The cells release signals that may help calm inflammation and support repair.

Will I need to stop working?

Plan a couple of restful days at the hotel, one elevator away, with your coordinator checking on you. Your specialist maps out your return to work in your online consultation, and your coordinator handles your trip home.

Is this approved by the FDA?

Stem cell therapy is not approved by the FDA, but in Colombia it is legal, and that is where your treatment takes place, performed by specialist physicians.

Is there an age limit?

There's no fixed age limit. What matters is your overall health and the condition of your spine, reviewed in your online consultation.

Does it work for sciatica?

Many patients with sciatica have a herniated disc inflaming or pressing on a nerve root. Applying cells at the source aims to calm that inflammation and support repair. A specialist reviews your imaging, so your plan is designed for your case.

What imaging do I need to send?

A recent MRI is ideal, because X-rays alone rarely show disc problems. Send what you have on WhatsApp and a specialist tells you whether anything else is needed — before you spend anything.

What can you expect?

Every body responds at its own pace; improvement is usually expected at 4–6 months. What you can expect is the quality of the cells, the precision of the application, and a medical team with you the whole way.
