Your hair cycle

It starts at the root. So does the treatment.

Every hair grows from a follicle, and the tiny cluster of cells at its base — the dermal papilla — tells it when to grow. Here is where the cells go to work, and how the months unfold.

1

Where you are now

A living follicle

Thinning usually means follicles cycling shorter and finer, with an irritated, poorly nourished scalp around them. A living follicle is what the cells need to work with.

2

Day 1 · Medellín

Cells and PRP at the root

Stem cells and your own PRP are placed into the scalp. They aim to calm follicular inflammation, support the dermal papilla and improve the scalp’s microcirculation.

Quick answers

Before you book.

How do the cells work on my hair?

The cells work with the follicles you have: they aim to help your hair grow faster or thicker. Your doctor looks at your case first, so your plan is designed for you before you book a flight.

How many days do I need in Medellín?

One day, with one night at the hotel in the same building as the treatment center. Your coordinator arranges airport pickup and every transfer.

Is the application comfortable?

Most patients find it comfortable: it is done under local anesthesia. The application is by mesotherapy: small injections, like those used for Botox.

Where do I rest after the treatment?

In your room at the 4-star hotel, which is in the same building as the treatment center. Your doctor gives you your care instructions in writing, and your coordinator answers questions on WhatsApp, in English.

When do I see a result?

Hair grows slowly: results are expected between three and six months, and every scalp responds at its own pace. To keep supporting the follicles, your medicated spray goes home with you for six months, included in your price.

What is included in the price?

The stem cells and your own PRP, two online consultations with your aesthetic doctor, a medicated spray for six months, one night at a 4-star hotel with the treatment center in the same building, airport pickup and all transfers, and a bilingual patient care coordinator. Flights and meals are not included.

What is checked before I travel?

For your safety, your doctor reviews your health in your online consultation before you travel, including ruling out active cancer.

Can I do my face in the same trip?

Yes. Face and hair fit on the same day, also with fractional CO2 laser, starting from $4,500. Tell your coordinator on WhatsApp for a combined quote.

Is this approved by the FDA?

Stem cell therapy is not approved by the FDA, but in Colombia it is legal.
