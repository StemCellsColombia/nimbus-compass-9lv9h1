About the treatment

What kind of stem cells are used?

Mesenchymal stem cells from Wharton’s jelly, the tissue of the umbilical cord, at Passage 3–4: young cells, grown just long enough for a full treatment dose, while many clinics grow theirs to Passage 5 and beyond. A passage is each round the cells spend growing in the lab. Every vial comes with its own quality certificate. Why Passage 3–4 matters.

Why come to Colombia instead of the US?

In the US, stem cells expanded in a laboratory can only be used inside clinical trials authorized by the FDA. In Colombia the law allows them in treatment, applied by specialists, all-inclusive and at a fraction of the US price. Stem cell therapy is not approved by the FDA, but in Colombia it is legal.

Does the application hurt?

It is done under local anesthesia or sedation, depending on your protocol, so you stay comfortable throughout.

Is it safe?

Applications are performed in a fully equipped operating room, with ultrasound or fluoroscopic guidance, by specialists. Your specialist reviews your individual health in your consultation before anything is booked.

How is this different from PRP or cortisone?

Cortisone and PRP are often used to manage symptoms for a while. Mesenchymal stem cells release signals that may help calm inflammation and support the body’s own repair processes. Your specialist explains what to expect in your case.

Is this approved by the FDA?

Stem cell therapy is not approved by the FDA, but in Colombia it is legal. It complements your regular medical care, and every case is reviewed by a specialist first.

When will I see results?

Every body responds at its own pace. Cells work gradually, from the inside: for joints and spine, improvement is usually expected at 4 to 6 months, and your follow-up consultations track your progress.

Will I need more than one application?

Some conditions improve with a single application; others may need more in the future. For joints, the team assesses your progress at about six months before recommending anything else. For systemic conditions, a second visit 6 to 8 weeks after the first is usually recommended.

Your trip

How many days do I need in Medellín?

Most protocols are one day of treatment with one night at the hotel included; for joints and spine the doctors recommend staying at least two days after the application. The intravenous protocols take four days and the intrathecal protocol five. The hotel is included. See your journey.

What is included in the price?

Your treatment, doctor consultations before and after, a 4-star hotel in the same building as the treatment center, airport pickup and every transfer, and a bilingual coordinator. Flights and meals are not included. See starting prices.

Is Medellín safe?

Your hotel and the treatment center are in El Poblado, one of the city’s most international neighborhoods, and your coordinator arranges every transfer. More about Medellín.

Do I need to speak Spanish?

No. You are looked after in English from your first message to your flight home.

Can I bring a companion?

Of course. Many patients travel with a partner or a family member, and your coordinator helps you plan for it.

After your treatment

What does recovery look like?

You rest at the hotel, one elevator away from the treatment center, with written care instructions and your coordinator a message away.

Can I take anti-inflammatories afterwards?

After the application you take only the medication your doctor recommends and skip anti-inflammatories (NSAIDs), so the cells can do their work.

What follow-up do I get?

Online consultations with the same team once you are home, included in your price.
