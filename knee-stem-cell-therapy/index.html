Knee FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Can stem cell therapy help me avoid a knee replacement?

For many patients with mild to moderate osteoarthritis, cartilage wear or meniscus damage, stem cell therapy is a way to treat the knee before an irreversible surgery. It isn't right for every knee — severe, collapsed joints may still need a replacement — so a specialist reviews your imaging first and tells you honestly.

Am I a candidate if I've been told I'm "bone on bone"?

Possibly. Mild to moderate wear responds best and advanced cases are less predictable. Send us your X-ray or MRI and we'll tell you what's realistic for your knee — before you spend anything.

How much does stem cell therapy for knees cost?

All-inclusive knee treatment in Medellín starts from $3,990 USD, including the cells, the guided application in the operating room, hotel, transfers and follow-up. Comparable treatments in the US typically run $20,000+. Your exact price is confirmed after your consultation.

Does it hurt?

The application is done under mild sedation or local anesthesia, so you stay comfortable. Afterward, expect some soreness and stiffness for a few days; you receive clear post-care instructions, and we are one message away.

When will I see results?

Improvement builds gradually, typically between 4 and 6 months after your treatment, and some patients keep improving at 8 months. The cells release signals that may help calm inflammation and support repair.

How long do I need to stay in Medellín?

Most knee treatments are done in a single day; some plans add a second day, and your quote tells you exactly which one is yours. We recommend staying at least 2 days after your application to rest at the hotel, and you can add extra nights to enjoy the city.

Can you treat both knees on the same trip?

Yes. Both knees — or a knee plus another joint — can be treated on the same procedure day. Your quote reflects exactly what's included.

Is stem cell therapy better than PRP or hyaluronic acid for knees?

They work differently. PRP uses growth factors from your own blood and hyaluronic acid adds lubrication; neither contains living cells. Stem cell therapy delivers 50 million young cells that may help calm inflammation and support tissue repair inside the joint.

Can stem cells treat a torn meniscus without surgery?

Many degenerative and partial meniscus tears can be treated without arthroscopy. The cells may help calm inflammation and support tissue repair. A specialist reviews your MRI to confirm whether your tear is a good fit.

Is there an age limit?

There's no fixed age limit. What matters is your overall health and the condition of the joint, reviewed in your online consultation.

Is it legal and safe?

Stem cell therapy is not approved by the FDA, but in Colombia it is legal. Your application is performed by specialist physicians in a fully-equipped operating room, under ultrasound guidance.

Is it guaranteed?

No honest treatment center guarantees results. What we guarantee is the quality of the cells, the precision of the application, and that we'll be with you the whole way.
