Shoulder FAQ

Your questions, answered.

Can stem cell therapy help me avoid shoulder replacement surgery?

For many patients with rotator cuff tears, frozen shoulder or mild to moderate shoulder osteoarthritis, stem cell therapy is a way to treat the shoulder before an irreversible surgery. A specialist reviews your imaging first, so your plan is designed for your shoulder.

Can stem cells help a torn rotator cuff heal without surgery?

Many partial and degenerative rotator cuff tears can be treated without surgery. The cells may help calm inflammation and support tendon and tissue repair. A specialist reviews your MRI, so your plan is designed for your tear.

Does it work for frozen shoulder?

Many patients with frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis) may see reduced pain and more movement as inflammation calms and the joint capsule heals. Your online consultation designs the plan for your case.

How much does stem cell therapy for the shoulder cost?

All-inclusive shoulder treatment in Medellín starts from $3,990 USD, including the cells, the guided application in the operating room, hotel, transfers and follow-up. Comparable treatments in the US typically run $20,000+. Your exact price is confirmed after your consultation.

Does it hurt?

The application is done under mild sedation or local anesthesia, so you stay comfortable. Afterward, you rest at the hotel, one elevator away, with your coordinator checking on you and clear post-care instructions in hand.

When will I see results?

Improvement builds gradually, typically between 4 and 6 months after your treatment, and some patients keep improving at 8 months. The cells release signals that may help calm inflammation and support repair.

How long do I need to stay in Medellín?

Most shoulder treatments are done in a single day; some plans add a second day, and your quote tells you exactly which one is yours. The doctors recommend staying at least 2 days after your application to rest at the hotel, and you can add extra nights to enjoy the city.

Can you treat both shoulders, or a shoulder and another joint, on the same trip?

Yes. Both shoulders — or a shoulder plus another joint — can be treated on the same procedure day. Your quote reflects exactly what's included.

Is stem cell therapy better than PRP or cortisone for the shoulder?

They work differently. Cortisone numbs pain temporarily, and PRP uses growth factors from your own blood; neither contains living cells. Stem cell therapy delivers 50 million young cells that may help calm inflammation and support tissue repair inside the joint.

Is there an age limit?

There's no fixed age limit. What matters is your overall health and the condition of the joint, reviewed in your online consultation.

Is it legal and safe?

Stem cell therapy is not approved by the FDA, but in Colombia it is legal. Your application is performed by specialist physicians in a fully-equipped operating room, under ultrasound guidance, by a team whose lead doctors have performed more than 8,000 procedures.

What can you expect?

Every body responds at its own pace; improvement is usually expected at 4–6 months. What you can expect is the quality of the cells, the precision of the application, and a medical team with you the whole way.
