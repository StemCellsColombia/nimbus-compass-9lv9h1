About Dr. Salgado

What Dr. Salgado does for you.

Dr. Salgado is a physician from Universidad CES, with a special interest in orthopedics and surgery. During his training he rotated through internal medicine, surgery, pediatrics and orthopedics, and completed a clinical rotation at Ochsner Medical Center in the United States.

At Stem Cells Colombia he leads the online consultations for joint and spine patients, before and after treatment. He speaks Spanish, English and French.
