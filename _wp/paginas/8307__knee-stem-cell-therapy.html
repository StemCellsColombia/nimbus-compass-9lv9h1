Is stem cell therapy better than PRP or hyaluronic acid for knees?

They work differently. PRP uses growth factors from your own blood and hyaluronic acid adds lubrication; neither contains living cells. Stem cell therapy delivers 50 million young cells that may help calm inflammation and support tissue repair inside the joint.

Can stem cells treat a torn meniscus without surgery?

Many degenerative and partial meniscus tears can be treated without arthroscopy. The cells may help calm inflammation and support tissue repair. A specialist reviews your MRI, so your plan is designed for your tear.

Is there an age limit?

There's no fixed age limit. What matters is your overall health and the condition of the joint, reviewed in your online consultation.

Is it legal and safe?

Stem cell therapy is not approved by the FDA, but in Colombia it is legal. Your application is performed by specialist physicians in a fully-equipped operating room, under ultrasound guidance, by a team whose lead doctors have performed more than 8,000 procedures.

What can you expect?

Every body responds at its own pace; improvement is usually expected at 4–6 months. What you can expect is the quality of the cells, the precision of the application, and a medical team with you the whole way.
