About Dr. Reyes

What Dr. Reyes does for you.

Dr. Reyes leads the stem cell applications at Stem Cells Colombia. She is an anesthesiologist from Universidad CES with a fellowship in pain and palliative care, and since 2019 she has worked in interventional pain at INCODOL in Medellín, where she places injections every day guided by ultrasound and fluoroscopy.

Together with Dr. Toro she performs the applications in joints, spine and the intrathecal route. She teaches pain medicine to fellowship physicians at Universidad CES, is certified in stem cell application by ISCCA and accredited in interventional pain techniques by FEDELAT. She also volunteers as an anesthesiologist with Operation Smile.
